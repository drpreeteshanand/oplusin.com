An Indian technology and infrastructure company built for how government actually buys.

Oplus Innovation Private Limited was incorporated on 30 March 2022 and is recognised under Startup India (DPIIT) and registered as an MSME (Udyam). We work with departments, districts, PSUs, universities and corporates across Bihar, eastern India and beyond.

We design, assemble and test drones, robotics and electronic systems in India; deliver aerial thermal and infrared inspection; build IoT, surveillance and smart-city systems; supply and integrate ICT, IT, audio-visual and office equipment through GeM; and execute solar, waste-management, sports-infrastructure, office and coworking projects. Every engagement ends with installation, training and a support commitment.

  • AirXMedic — medicine, vaccine and sample delivery; telemedicine support

Thermal & Infrared Inspection

Radiometric aerial thermography using uncooled VOx sensors (640 × 512, 7.5–14 µm, ≤ 50 mK) with synchronised ≥ 20 MP visible imaging. Every survey closes with a report identifying hotspots, cold-spots and temperature readings.

  • Solar plant thermography — hotspot, diode-failure and string-fault mapping
  • Substation, transformer, panel and transmission-line audits
  • Roof and façade heat-loss surveys; boiler, pipeline and insulation audits
  • Fire-line monitoring, search & rescue, night surveillance
  • Turnkey Made-in-India thermal systems with 5-year on-site warranty and 3-day training

For power and solar developers, DISCOMs, industrial plants, building owners, disaster management and fire services.

Office & Coworking Space Management, Software & Asset Monetisation

Government departments hold large office estates — floors, halls, guest houses, training centres — that are under-used, costly to run and managed on paper. We bring software to run the office, operations to manage the space, and models to turn idle assets into revenue. Our own facility, Oplus Cowork, is the live reference site.

  • Government Office Management System — room and hall booking, visitor e-pass, attendance and access, asset register, complaint ticketing, energy monitoring, MIS dashboards; e-Office integrable
  • Coworking & facility platform — memberships, online booking and payments, smart-lock access, GST invoicing, occupancy analytics; white-labelled for institutions
  • Managed operations — front desk, housekeeping, security coordination, IT and maintenance under one SLA
  • Asset monetisation — idle property audit → revenue model → coworking floors, incubators, training hubs, rentable halls → build-operate / revenue-share / lease

For state secretariats and directorates, district administrations, housing and industrial development boards, universities, PSUs and start-up missions.
