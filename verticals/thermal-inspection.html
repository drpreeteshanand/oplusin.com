05 · Warranty, support & timelines

Service commitments

Standard commitments

  • Warranty: up to 5 years comprehensive on-site for thermal imaging systems (as per bid); sensor re-calibration included
  • Response: remote support same day; on-site within 24 hours, resolution within 48 hours
  • Data: raw radiometric data handed over; hosted on-premise or in government-approved cloud as required
  • AMC: preventive maintenance calendar with quarterly reports

Typical delivery timelines

  • Turnkey thermal system supply: 4–8 weeks from order
  • Site survey (per plant, up to 10 MW): 1–3 days; report within 5 working days
  • Substation / line audit: 1–2 days per site
  • Demonstration flight: within 7 days
