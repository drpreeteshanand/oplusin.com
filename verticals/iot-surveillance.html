05 · Warranty, support & timelines

Service commitments

Standard commitments

  • Warranty: OEM warranty on devices (typically 1–3 years) plus installation warranty; extendable per bid
  • Response: helpdesk 24×7; on-site within 24 hours; critical-camera restoration within 48 hours
  • Spares: critical spares stocked regionally for the AMC term
  • Security: firmware patches and password rotation as part of AMC

Typical delivery timelines

  • Small sites (≤ 50 cameras): 3–6 weeks
  • Campus / town (50–500 devices): 8–16 weeks
  • City-scale or multi-campus: phased over 3–9 months
  • Site survey & DPR: 1–3 weeks
