04 · Deliverables

What is handed over on every supply order

Hardware

  • Airframe / platform with serial number & UIN
  • Batteries (2–4 sets), smart charger, cables
  • Ground-control station / controller and tablet
  • Payload (spray kit, camera, drop mechanism) as ordered
  • Transport case, propeller set, basic spares kit

Documentation

  • Pre-delivery inspection & flight-test report
  • User manual, maintenance schedule, SOP booklet
  • Warranty card and AMC schedule
  • Datasheets, battery safety certificates, local-content declaration
  • Software / app licences and credentials

People & process

  • Installation, commissioning and acceptance test at site
  • 3-day operator & maintenance training with certificates
  • Joint sign-off on acceptance checklist
  • Helpdesk contact, escalation matrix and service calendar
  • Quarterly health-check during warranty
05 · Warranty, support & timelines

Service commitments

Standard commitments

  • Warranty: 12–24 months comprehensive on-site (extendable up to 5 years as per bid); repair / replacement, firmware updates and preventive checks included
  • Response: remote support same day; on-site response within 24 hours, resolution within 48 hours at district headquarters
  • Spares: availability for 10 years on manufactured platforms; consumables (propellers, nozzles, batteries) ex-stock
  • AMC: comprehensive / non-comprehensive, with uptime SLA and quarterly reports

Typical delivery timelines

  • Standard platforms (AirXKrishi, inspection drone): 3–6 weeks from order
  • Custom payload integration or large fleets: 8–12 weeks
  • Demonstration / pilot flight: within 7 days of request
  • Service contracts (spraying, survey): mobilisation within 7–10 days of work order
  • Training batch: 3 days on-site, scheduled with delivery
