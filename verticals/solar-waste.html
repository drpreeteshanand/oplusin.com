05 · Warranty, support & timelines

Service commitments

Standard commitments

  • Warranty: 25-year module performance, 5–10-year inverter, 5-year workmanship (as per bid)
  • Response: remote monitoring alerts; on-site within 48 hours
  • O&M: quarterly cleaning / preventive maintenance, annual drone thermography
  • Guarantee: performance-ratio commitment with reporting

Typical delivery timelines

  • Rooftop up to 100 kW: 4–8 weeks (plus DISCOM approvals)
  • Rooftop 100–500 kW: 8–14 weeks
  • Ground-mount MW-scale: 4–6 months
  • Street lights / pumps: 3–6 weeks per lot
