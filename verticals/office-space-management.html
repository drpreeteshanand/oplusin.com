Vertical 09 · Products & services

Office & Coworking Space Management, Software & Asset Monetisation

Government departments hold large office estates — floors, halls, guest houses, training centres — that are under-used and managed on paper. We bring software to run the office, operations to manage the space, and models to turn idle assets into revenue. Our own facility, Oplus Cowork, is the live reference site.
