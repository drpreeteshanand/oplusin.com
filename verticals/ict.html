05 · Warranty, support & timelines

Service commitments

Standard commitments

  • Warranty: OEM on-site warranty (3–5 years for computers as per bid) plus our installation warranty
  • Response: helpdesk same day; on-site within 24–48 hours
  • Spares: OEM spares through authorised channel; loaner units for critical sites
  • AMC: comprehensive / non-comprehensive with SLA

Typical delivery timelines

  • Catalogue supply via GeM: 1–3 weeks
  • Lab / smart-classroom / AV room: 3–6 weeks
  • Campus network / server room: 6–12 weeks
  • Custom software: 8–16 weeks (phased)
