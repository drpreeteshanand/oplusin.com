05 · Warranty, support & timelines

Service commitments

Standard commitments

  • Warranty: 1-year comprehensive (extendable per bid); devices replaced, not just repaired
  • Response: remote within hours; on-site within 48 hours in Bihar / eastern India
  • Refills: monthly / quarterly, with IoT low-stock alerts
  • Reports: monthly usage and health-screening MIS to the department

Typical delivery timelines

  • Supply & installation: 2–4 weeks per lot (up to 50 units)
  • Large lots (100+): 6–10 weeks phased
  • Refill cycle: monthly / quarterly
  • Demonstration unit: within 7 days
