05 · Warranty, support & timelines

Service commitments

Standard commitments

  • Warranty: 3–5 years on synthetic surfaces; 1–2 years on equipment and lighting (per bid)
  • Defect liability: as per contract; repairs within 7 days of intimation
  • AMC: quarterly maintenance with reports
  • Safety: site safety plan and insurance for works

Typical delivery timelines

  • Single court: 3–5 weeks
  • Multi-court / turf ground: 6–12 weeks
  • Running track / multi-sport complex: 3–6 months
  • DPR: 2–3 weeks
